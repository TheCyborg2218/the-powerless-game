HOW TO PLAY

Walk the ranch with WASD or the arrow keys. Press E, SPACE or ENTER (or click) when a glowing marker is nearby to examine evidence or talk.

Each ranch hand will answer only THREE questions. Choose carefully.

Every answer becomes a card on the evidence board in your office. Drag cards, click to inspect. Talk to all three men to unlock the FINAL CASE.

There is no answer key. Build an interpretation from the evidence. Progress saves automatically.
