An Investigation of Power on a California Ranch

HOW TO PLAY

WASD / Arrows: move. E / Enter: interact. Space / Enter: advance dialogue. 1 / 2: choose a question. B: case board. Esc: menu.

Walk the ranch. Examine the places where the men live and work. Question the four hands, who each get three questions. Every answer is pinned to the case board. The board turns what you learn into inferences. When you have 12 pieces of evidence, the final case unlocks.
