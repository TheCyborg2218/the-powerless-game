HOW TO PLAY

Walk the ranch with WASD or the arrow keys. Press E, SPACE or ENTER (or click) when a glowing marker is nearby to examine a place or talk to a hand.

Each of the four ranch hands will answer only THREE questions. At each step you pick one of two. Choose carefully.

Everything you learn is pinned to the case board (press B, or step into your office). Click any card to inspect it. Collect 12 pieces of evidence to unlock the FINAL CASE.

There is no answer key. Build an interpretation from the evidence. Progress saves automatically.
